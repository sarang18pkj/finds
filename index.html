Ye ek sample list hai. Upar wala button dabao, har baar nayi list aayegi. Price app mein check karke hi video banao.

Kaunsa product chalega?

  • 3 second mein "wow" — kaam turant dikhe (before/after, satisfying moment).
  • Har ghar ki problem — tel girna, baal uljhna, jagah kam padna.
  • Price shock — "sirf ₹49!" screen par bada likho.
  • Haath se demo ho sake — chhota, rangeen, camera par saaf dikhe.
  • Khane-peene ki cheezein, dawai aur skin par lagane wale sasta chemical — avoid karo.

15–30 second ki video ka dhaancha

  1. 0–2 sec: Hook bolo + product haath mein + price screen par.
  2. 2–15 sec: Asli use karke dikhao. Ek close-up zaroor.
  3. Verdict: "Paisa vasool" ya "Mat lena" — imaandaari se. Flop wale products bhi dikhao; isi se trust banta hai.
  4. End: "Kaunsi app se liya — comment mein likho LINK."

Posting ka routine

  • Ek vertical video → Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts aur Facebook teeno par.
  • Roz 1 video. Shaam 7–9 baje sabse accha.
  • Chehra zaroori nahi — sirf haath aur awaaz bhi chalti hai.
  • Pehle ghante har comment ka jawab do.
  • Agar product free mila ho ya paise mile hon, video mein "Ad" ya "Gifted" likhna zaroori hai (ASCI rule).
  • Followers badhne ke baad affiliate links (Amazon Associates, EarnKaro) se kamai shuru kar sakte hain.
